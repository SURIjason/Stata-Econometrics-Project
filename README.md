# Stata-Econometrics-Project
Geopolitical Alignment and China's Trade Relationships
An empirical panel-data study investigating the relationship between geopolitical alignment, foreign direct investment, and bilateral trade shares with China, including the impact of COVID-19.
Stata
Panel Data
Fixed Effects
International Trade

Research methods
- Country fixed-effects regression with clustered robust standard errors
- Breusch–Pagan heteroskedasticity test
- Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) diagnostics
- COVID-19 interaction analysis
- Trade-share trend visualization
  
Key finding:

Geopolitical proximity and FDI were not statistically significant predictors of trade shares in your estimated model, while year effects captured substantial shifts during and after the pandemic.
